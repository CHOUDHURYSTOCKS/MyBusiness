India Grid Trust

NSE: INDIGRID
InvIT
Market Cap ₹14,500 Cr
Target Yield ~9.5%

Outlook: Top choice for income-seeking investors requiring consistent, high payout yields.

Taxation & Regulatory Note: Under SEBI regulations, REITs and InvITs must distribute at least 90% of Net Distributable Cash Flows (NDCF) to unitholders. Distributions are typically split into Dividend, Interest, Repayment of Debt, and Other Income. Tax treatment depends on the SPV tax regime chosen by the sponsor and the unitholder's applicable income tax slab.
